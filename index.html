THE COMMISSIONING JOURNEY

A bespoke automotive build is an emotional odyssey. Follow every milestone of your vehicle's metamorphosis.

01
MONTH 01

Private Consultation & Digital Twin Spec

You meet one-on-one with our Chief Aerodynamicist and Interior Master at our Stuttgart atelier. We build a photorealistic 3D CAD digital twin of your exact commission, down to the stitch thread color and carbon weave angle.

DELIVERABLE: PHOTOREALISTIC CAD PROTOTYPE & MATERIAL SAMPLES
02
MONTHS 02 - 03

Tear-Down & Laser Metrology NDT Scanning

Your donor vehicle is stripped down to the bare carbon or aluminum tub. Every joint undergoes non-destructive ultrasonic testing (NDT). High-precision laser scanners capture 100 million coordinate points to confirm structural alignment within 0.02mm.

DELIVERABLE: CHASSIS STRUCTURAL CERTIFICATE & NDT HEALTH REPORT
03
MONTHS 04 - 07

Engine Blueprinting & Autoclave Fabrication

The engine is rebuilt in our temperature-controlled clean room. Piston weights matched within 0.05 grams. New carbon bodywork is laid up by hand, vacuum bagged, and baked under 7 bar pressure. Exhaust headers are 3D-printed in Inconel 625.

DELIVERABLE: DYNO HORSEPOWER LOG & TIME-LAPSE BUILD REEL
04
MONTHS 08 - 10

Dyno Calibration & High-Speed Circuit Validation

500 kilometers of rigorous testing. Suspension geometry corner-weighted on laser scales with ballast matching your exact body weight. High-speed aero validation and acoustic sound-level tuning at Nürburgring Nordschleife.

DELIVERABLE: CIRCUIT TELEMETRY DATA & HIGH-SPEED VIDEO
05
MONTH 11 - 12

Private Circuit Handover & Monograph Delivery

Delivery takes place at a private track reserved exclusively for you, accompanied by our Chief Test Driver. You receive your bespoke billet key, customized flight luggage, and a hand-bound 200-page leather archive monograph detailing every serial number.
